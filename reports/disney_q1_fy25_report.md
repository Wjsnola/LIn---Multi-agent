# Disney Q1 FY25 Earnings — Research Note Summary

Executive summary

Our internal Q1 FY25 assessment of The Walt Disney Company was Positive / Outperform. The core thesis characterized Disney as a multi-pillar earnings recovery story driven by a clear DTC/streaming profit inflection, a studio/content rebound, and resilient profitability in Experiences. The note set a 12‑month target price of $115 (price at note ~$98.79), implying ~16% upside and valuing the company at about 19.2x FY26E EPS of ~$5.99. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)

Original research question / hypothesis

Is Disney’s recovery broadening and sustainable, led by a DTC/streaming profit inflection, a studio/content rebound, and resilient Experiences, such that the company merits a Positive / Outperform rating? (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)

Key findings

Rating and valuation

- Sentiment: Positive / Outperform. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)
- 12‑month target price: $115; price at note: ~$98.79, implying roughly 16% upside. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)
- Valuation basis: ~19.2x FY26E EPS of ~$5.99. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)

Operational and financial highlights (positives)

- Adjusted EPS rose 44% to $1.76. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)
- Segment operating income increased by about 31%. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)
- DTC operating income turned positive, reaching $293 million, helped by Disney+ ARPU and Hulu improvement. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)
- Entertainment/studio operating income nearly doubled to about $1.7 billion. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)
- Experiences remained resilient with operating income around $3.1 billion. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)
- ESPN/sports returned to profitability, aided by Star India deconsolidation and ad growth. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)

Main concerns and risks

- Free cash flow declined about 17% to $739 million. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)
- Capital expenditures roughly doubled to about $2.5 billion, driven partly by cruise/fleet investment. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)
- Potential risks include streaming churn or ARPU pressure, sports rights inflation and ad‑market risk at ESPN, parks attendance/macro/weather volatility, and elevated capex delaying free cash flow recovery. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)

Catalysts to watch

- Continued DTC profitability and ARPU gains. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)
- Strong film/content slate and improved content monetization. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)
- Better cost discipline and execution in Experiences. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)
- Further evidence of sustainable earnings recovery in subsequent quarters. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)

Conclusion

The Q1 FY25 assessment framed Disney as a broadening recovery across multiple pillars—DTC, studio/content, and Experiences—supporting a Positive / Outperform stance and a $115 12‑month target. Key near‑term risks center on cash flow, elevated capex, and sector‑specific pressures such as streaming churn and sports rights costs; catalysts for upside are further DTC profitability and strong content performance. (Source: Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025 — disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf)
