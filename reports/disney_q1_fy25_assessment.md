# Disney Q1 FY25 Assessment — Positive / Outperform

## Executive summary
Disney’s earnings recovery is broadening, led by a clear direct-to-consumer (DTC)/streaming profit inflection, a studio/content rebound, and resilient profitability in Experiences, supporting a positive Outperform view. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`). The firm was framed as a multi-pillar earnings recovery story with a 12-month target price of $115 versus a note price of about $98.79, implying roughly 16% upside. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`). The valuation basis cited was about 19.2x FY26E EPS of ~$5.99. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).

## Original research question / hypothesis
Hypothesis: Disney’s recovery is broadening and sustainable, driven by a DTC/streaming profit inflection, a studio/content rebound, and resilient Experiences profitability—positioning Disney as a multi-pillar earnings recovery story. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).

## Key findings

Rating and valuation
- Sentiment: Positive / Outperform. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).
- 12-month target price: $115. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).
- Note price: approximately $98.79, implying roughly 16% upside to the $115 target. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).
- Valuation basis: ~19.2x FY26E EPS of ~$5.99. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).

Operational and financial performance (Q1 FY25 highlights)
- Adjusted EPS rose 44% to $1.76. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).
- Segment operating income increased about 31%. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).
- DTC operating income turned positive, reaching $293 million, supported by Disney+ ARPU and Hulu improvement. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).
- Entertainment/studio operating income nearly doubled to about $1.7 billion. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).
- Experiences remained resilient with operating income around $3.1 billion. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).
- ESPN/sports returned to profitability, aided by Star India deconsolidation and ad growth. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).

Main concerns and risks
- Free cash flow declined about 17% to $739 million. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).
- Capital expenditures roughly doubled to about $2.5 billion, driven partly by cruise/fleet investment. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).
- Potential risks noted: streaming churn or ARPU pressure; sports rights inflation and ad-market risk at ESPN; parks attendance/macro/weather volatility; and elevated capex delaying FCF recovery. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).

Identified catalysts
- Continued DTC profitability and ARPU gains. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).
- Strong film/content slate and improved content monetization. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).
- Better cost discipline and execution in Experiences. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).
- Further evidence of sustainable earnings recovery in subsequent quarters. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).

## Conclusion
The internal Q1 FY25 assessment rated Disney Positive / Outperform, based on a multi-pillar recovery thesis supported by a DTC profit inflection, studio rebound, and resilient Experiences profitability, while noting near-term cash-flow and capex risks that could delay full FCF recovery. (Walt Disney Co. (DIS) | Q1 FY25 Earnings Review, Feb. 5, 2025, `disney_q1_fy25_research_note.pdf`).
